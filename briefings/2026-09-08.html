The portfolio becomes the unit of action

OBS Between 3 and 8 September 2026, public actors repeatedly assembled unlike capabilities into one governing object. The EU, Greenland, and Denmark paired a joint declaration with €200 million for connectivity, energy, critical materials, fisheries, education, and other services. UNESCO placed AI tutors, synthetic knowledge, digital sovereignty, literacy, and education as a common good inside one week of meetings. Space firms joined launch hardware to unusually large capital claims, while ecological research linked organism response to timing, prior exposure, and managed environments.

INF Briefing 098 asked where a named framework first acquires work-doing power. Today the problem moves one level deeper. A portfolio can contain individually credible components and still fail as a system because the couplings among them remain unspecified. Connectivity can serve local access or raw-material extraction. AI literacy can protect judgment or become a thin label attached to procurement. Finance can accelerate launch cadence or absorb risk without delivering it. The decisive object is therefore the interface: who has authority there, what information crosses it, and which component can stop the others.

HYP The field can release through three distinct paths. The portfolios may become mutually reinforcing systems, with transparent projects, budgets, rights, and measures. They may decompose into separately administered channels whose shared language masks weak coordination. Or a visible component may dominate the rest: connectivity organized around extraction, AI policy around vendor capability, launch finance around valuation, or climate assessment around publication rather than adaptation. The next evidence is specific enough to discriminate among those paths.

01 · Geopolitical

Partnership, Relief, and Strategic Capacity

4 developments
7 Sep 2026 · Nuuk, GreenlandSource

The EU and Greenland turn Arctic partnership into a funded portfolio

On 7 September, the EU, Greenland, and Denmark signed a joint declaration in Nuuk alongside a €200 million Global Gateway package. The official account joins connectivity, energy, critical materials, fisheries, education, housing, tourism, and small-business support. The signed declaration and named funding move the relationship beyond diplomatic sentiment. They do not yet show which projects are new, which actors control them, or how Greenlandic priorities will govern implementation.

The geopolitical effect depends on those details. If financing, permits, local counterpart commitments, and procurement rights become visible together, the package can thicken Greenland's room for choice while giving Europe durable Arctic capacity. If security and extraction priorities dominate, partnership language may conceal an unequal allocation of control. The evidence supports neither conclusion yet. Project lists, concession terms, and local approval mechanisms are the next discriminants.

8 Sep 2026 · China and NepalSource

China's fourth Nepal shipment makes relief a logistical relationship

China reported sending a fourth batch of emergency relief supplies to Nepal on 8 September. The listed equipment included transport, lighting, and mortuary capabilities, while Chinese organizations had established temporary shelters and basic medical services. A Nepalese report said the shipment arrived in Kathmandu aboard a Y-20A aircraft. Four successive deliveries establish operational repetition, not merely a pledge.

Repeated relief can remain a conventional disaster response. It can also create a practical diplomatic channel through aircraft access, warehousing, technical support, and coordination with local authorities. Nepalese delivery records, valuations, distribution data, and evidence of equipment use will show how much of the relationship survives beyond the visible arrival. The relevant distinction is between assistance delivered to a sovereign system and a parallel capacity that begins to organize the response around itself.

7 Sep 2026 · Southern LebanonSource

A lethal strike keeps the ceasefire form separate from the kinetic channel

Lebanon’s Health Ministry reported on 7 September that Israeli strikes killed one person in Rayhan and wounded ten people in two Nabatieh locations. The official casualty record establishes a lethal incident and its reported toll. It does not establish the targets, the military rationale, or whether the sequence marks a broader operational shift.

The event nevertheless continues a structural separation already visible in the region: a diplomatic or ceasefire framework can persist while the kinetic channel produces casualties. Channel Decomposition is the careful description because the institutional form and the physical sequence are separately observable. Ceasefire Acceleration would require stronger evidence that the nominal pause is actively speeding a wider transformation. Geolocated reporting, military accounts, and subsequent strike cadence will distinguish a localized exchange from a change in the conflict pattern.

7 Sep 2026 · Eastern PacificSource

SOUTHCOM reports intercepting and sinking a refueling vessel

U.S. Southern Command said that Joint Task Force Western Hemisphere intercepted a floating refueling station in international waters in the eastern Pacific on 7 September, removed the people aboard for transfer to Ecuadorian authorities, and then sank the vessel. SOUTHCOM attributed the platform to illicit drug trafficking and Los Choneros. The release verifies the U.S. military action and its official rationale. It does not independently establish the cargo, affiliation, intelligence basis, or legal theory.

The operation makes maritime logistics a target rather than treating only cargo vessels as the actionable unit. That can disrupt a network by constraining endurance and range, but the public record is too thin to measure the effect. Crew-transfer records, evidence released in any proceeding, subsequent interdictions, and changes in refueling patterns would distinguish a demonstrable logistical break from an isolated removal. The institutional question is whether military interdiction, criminal process, and partner-state custody form one accountable chain.

◉ Deep dive · When does a portfolio increase sovereignty?

Greenland's package can widen choice or reorganize dependence

A portfolio is often treated as diversified because it contains several sectors. That is insufficient. Connectivity, hydropower, critical-material projects, fisheries, housing, tourism, and education can share one budget while pulling authority in different directions. Satellite links may strengthen local services and make remote extraction easier. Hydropower can lower community energy costs and support energy-intensive industry. Training can build local competence or prepare labor for projects governed elsewhere.

The package therefore has to be read as a configuration. Greenlandic approval rights, ownership shares, data control, procurement, and the sequencing of public-service and extractive projects determine whether the same €200 million expands local options. A common funding envelope does not make the components mutually reinforcing by itself.

Sovereignty grows when an actor can refuse, redirect, and recombine resources. It narrows when access to one benefit requires accepting a bundle whose gatekeeping sits elsewhere. Commons Enclosure should be invoked only if a shared resource, a literal access gate, and a gatekeeper with exclusion or fee-setting power all become visible. The present evidence supports a partnership under construction, not that stronger conclusion.

Question for the record: Which first disclosed term would most sharply distinguish capability transferred to Greenland from capability installed in Greenland?

02 · Technological

Cadence, Capital, and Knowledge Infrastructure

3 developments
5 Sep 2026 · Andøya, NorwaySource

Five deployed payloads make the launch test more than a flight test

Exolaunch's mission record says Spectrum deployed five small satellites and an experiment on 5 September. Payload delivery matters because ascent alone can leave the commercial function untested. Separation, target orbit, and customer payload handling join vehicle performance to the downstream service customers actually purchased.

The remaining question is repeatability. A launch business needs manufacturing throughput, range access, regulatory continuity, payload integration, and customers whose schedules can tolerate the provider's cadence. One success can attract orders without proving that configuration. The next launches will reveal whether the system advances by learning or pauses for redesign. The distinction is a Verification-Mode Asymmetry: a public mission demonstrates what executed, while reliability emerges only through a sequence that no single success can verify.

8 Sep 2026 · United StatesSource

Stoke announces a $1 billion initial close around full reusability

Stoke Space announced on 8 September an initial closing of a $1 billion Series E round to develop fully reusable launch vehicles. The wording is precise enough to matter: an initial close is a financing event, while the announced purpose is a technical program whose milestones remain ahead. The source does not disclose in the bounded record how much cash is immediately available, the complete investor syndicate, preferences, or engineering conditions attached to later funding.

Capital of this scale can buy test cadence, manufacturing capacity, and tolerance for failure. It can also make valuation and schedule narratives harder to separate from technical evidence. The next observables are funded amount, test articles, engine and stage results, and a dated launch path. Optionality Arbitrage applies if investors are buying scarce future access whose value rises under launch constraint. It does not establish that the vehicle will work.

8–11 Sep 2026 · ParisSource

UNESCO treats synthetic knowledge as education infrastructure

UNESCO's Digital Learning Week began in Paris on 8 September. Its published program includes AI tutors, agentic systems, synthetic content, digital sovereignty, and community-led alternatives. Bringing these topics into one forum treats AI as part of the knowledge infrastructure through which students encounter claims, receive feedback, and develop judgment.

The technical object is broader than a model. It includes training data, interfaces, procurement, assessment, teacher authority, and the means by which synthetic material can be challenged. Capability Opacity arises when a system can generate instruction whose quality students and institutions cannot inspect. Governance Vacuum would require a named institution, a missing capability, and an observable consequence. Here UNESCO is the institution and a ministerial process exists; whether it supplies monitoring or only language remains open until the statement and implementation measures appear.

03 · Economic

Composition Beneath the Headline

3 developments
31 Aug–1 Sep 2026 · ChinaSource

China's 49.8 headline separates from orders, output, and employment

Briefing 097 recorded China's August manufacturing PMI at 49.8, below the expansion threshold for a second month. The official release adds a useful decomposition: production stood at 50.4 and new orders at 50.6, while employment registered 48.7. The headline has not moved; the analytical resolution has. Output and demand can sit marginally above 50 while factory employment remains in contraction.

This combination blocks a scalar reading of “manufacturing weakness.” Firms may be meeting orders through productivity, hours, inventory, or automation without adding labor. Alternatively, small movements around 50 may be noise within a broadly stagnant system. September's official PMI, export orders, industrial output, and employment will discriminate between stabilization with labor thinning and a short-lived divergence. The repeated 49.8 is continuity evidence, explicitly explained, rather than a new release.

8 Sep 2026 · South AfricaSource

South Africa contracts 0.2% as several sectors weaken together

Statistics South Africa reported on 8 September that real GDP declined 0.2% quarter on quarter in the second quarter of 2026. The agency identified mining, trade, manufacturing, imports, and weak investment among the main contributors. Because the figure joins production and expenditure channels, it carries more information than a single-sector shock.

One quarter does not establish a durable contraction regime. Inventories, revisions, and sector timing can change the diagnosis, and Statistics South Africa has an October rebasing and benchmarking release ahead. The more consequential question is whether weak investment and employment-sensitive sectors reinforce one another. If subsequent data show falling fixed investment, weak hiring, and limited household demand, a Threshold Cascade becomes plausible. If revisions or third-quarter activity reverse the decline, the 0.2% reading will remain a narrow interruption rather than a system transition.

3 Sep 2026 · Bangkok, ThailandSource

Thailand's next growth engine is framed as firm dynamism

A World Bank report launched in Bangkok on 3 September frames Thailand's path toward high-income status around productivity, upgrading, and greater dynamism across firms, people, and regions. The framing moves attention from aggregate capital accumulation toward entry, diffusion, skills, and spatial allocation.

The reform vocabulary is familiar; implementation capacity is the discriminant. Firm dynamism can be suppressed by financing gaps, regulatory barriers, weak diffusion from frontier firms, or regional infrastructure. Each mechanism implies a different intervention and observable. Entry and exit rates, productivity dispersion, technology adoption, and regional convergence should move if the diagnosis becomes policy. If the report produces no assigned authority, budget, or dated reform sequence, Process as Destination may describe the policy conversation: analysis continues because agreement on the operative bottleneck does not.

◉ Deep dive · Capital can accelerate cadence or thicken the story

Space finance should be evaluated against a milestone ladder

Isar's completed orbital delivery and Stoke's $1 billion financing announcement sit on different rungs. One is evidence that a particular vehicle transported particular payloads on a dated mission. The other is evidence that investors and a company completed at least an initial financing close around a future vehicle. Combining them into a single “commercial space acceleration” story would erase the type of evidence each supplies.

The right comparison is a ladder: capital available, hardware built, subsystem tested, integrated vehicle tested, payload delivered, flight repeated, and cadence sustained. Money can shorten intervals between those rungs. It can also support parallel development paths that preserve options when one design fails.

It cannot substitute for a rung. This matters because private markets price the whole future sequence before public evidence reaches it. Optionality Arbitrage may be rational when launch access is scarce, yet the option's value and the vehicle's demonstrated reliability are different variables. Investors can be right about scarcity and wrong about the firm.

Research prompt: Track the elapsed time and information gain between each announced financing close and the next independently observable engineering milestone.

04 · Scientific

Signals, Memory, and Timing

3 developments
8 Sep 2026 · Australia and global astronomySource

A white-dwarf catalogue tests the assumptions beneath cosmic acceleration

The University of Queensland reported on 8 September that a comprehensive catalogue of exploding white-dwarf stars provides new evidence relevant to dark-energy models and cosmic acceleration. The bounded institutional report supports the existence and intended use of the dataset. It does not justify a claim that the standard cosmological model has been overturned.

Type Ia supernovae matter because cosmological inference depends on using observed brightness and calibration to estimate distance. A larger catalogue can narrow uncertainty while exposing systematic differences that smaller samples hid. The next evidence is methodological: catalogue construction, calibration assumptions, selection effects, and independent use of the distance ladder. Verification-Mode Asymmetry applies if the headline interpretation moves faster than specialist replication. The scientific gain may be a smaller model space rather than a dramatic replacement of the model.

3 Sep 2026 · Saudi ArabiaSource

Prior pathogen exposure appears to prime a later coral response

KAUST reported on 3 September that laboratory exposure to coral-pathogen cells appeared to prime corals for a more effective response to later infection. The finding describes a response under controlled conditions. It does not establish durable immune memory across coral species, pathogen strains, or natural reefs.

The result matters because conservation interventions often assume that protection means reducing exposure. Priming introduces another possible mechanism: a bounded exposure could change later response. That possibility carries obvious limits. Laboratory conditions may omit heat stress, microbial communities, nutrient variation, and repeated infection. Field replication, duration, fitness costs, and species breadth will discriminate an adaptable response from a narrow experimental effect. Observation-Action Decoupling would appear if the response can be measured but cannot be converted into a safe conservation practice.

4 Sep 2026 · AustraliaSource

Sponge larvae use sunset-associated cues before metamorphosis

A University of Queensland study reported on 4 September that environmental signals associated with sunset help sea-sponge larvae prepare for metamorphosis despite the absence of a nervous system. The careful claim concerns timing and response. It does not imply cognition or a general circadian architecture.

The finding widens the set of mechanisms through which organisms can coordinate a major transition. Light intensity, wavelength, temperature, tides, and correlated chemical signals can each carry information at sunset. Experimental controls must separate them. The conceptual lesson is narrower than anthropomorphic language suggests: complex timing can be distributed across molecular and environmental couplings rather than centralized in a nervous system. That makes the environment part of the mechanism. Change the cue architecture and the developmental sequence may change even when the organism's internal components remain intact.

The sixtieth Literacy Day joins people, planet, and prosperity

UNESCO scheduled the global International Literacy Day celebration and literacy-prize ceremony for 8–9 September in Chiapas under the theme “Literacy for people, the planet and prosperity.” The sixtieth anniversary gives the observance institutional durability. Avistar Rio took place on 5–6 September at Rio de Janeiro's botanical garden. Photography, tourism, and journalism can increase participation, but they can also bias attention toward charismatic species and accessible locations. Submitted observations, validation procedures, database entry, and later research use will show whether the event expanded scientific perception. 06 · Environmental and Ecological

Assessment Becomes Action Only Through a Managed Interface

3 developments
3 Sep 2026 · CanadaSource

Canada updates the physical evidence before the adaptation ledger

Canada published its 2026 Changing Climate Report on 3 September. The official description covers temperature, precipitation, snow and ice, freshwater, sea level, wildfire risk, and climate extremes, drawing on peer-reviewed evidence and expert participation. The assessment strengthens the public observation layer. It does not itself allocate adaptation money or establish local implementation.

The distinction matters because a national synthesis can improve shared diagnosis while leaving responsibility fragmented across federal, provincial, municipal, Indigenous, and private actors. Governance Vacuum requires a named institution and missing capacity with observable consequences; publication alone does not meet that test. The next assessment products, funded actions, infrastructure standards, and local plans will show whether knowledge changes exposure. If impacts become more precisely described while delivery remains static, Capacity Hollowing would be the stronger diagnosis.

1 Sep 2026 · JapanSource

Japan's amended invasive-species ordinance reaches its effective date

Japan's Environment Ministry listed the promulgation and enforcement on 1 September of an amendment to the legal regime governing ecological damage from specified invasive alien species. The dated effective action distinguishes this item from a proposal. The bounded record does not yet establish the amended species list, enforcement powers, or compliance burden.

Invasive-species governance joins biological timing to administrative timing. Authorities must detect, classify, and intervene before a population crosses a threshold that makes eradication impractical. A legal effective date can strengthen that chain only if agencies and local governments receive usable lists, authority, resources, and reporting duties. Deadline Revelation applies because implementation exposes whether those capacities were ready. Enforcement guidance, inspections, removal actions, and ecological monitoring will show whether the amendment changes the physical system.

7 Sep 2026 · Mekong Delta, VietnamSource

A Mekong rice partnership reports 10% higher profits and 5% lower costs

A University of Queensland-led partnership reported on 7 September that participating Vietnamese smallholder rice farmers increased profits by 10% and reduced production costs by 5%. The result identifies a promising joint movement in income and cost. The source record supplied here does not establish sample design, comparison farms, input-price assumptions, or persistence across seasons.

Profit and cost improvements can arise from yield, input reduction, price, selection, or unusually favorable conditions. Those mechanisms have different scaling implications. A practice that reduces fertilizer or water while maintaining yield may strengthen both household and ecological resilience. A result driven by participant selection may not travel. Comparisons across several seasons, distributions among farms, and explicit baselines are therefore essential. The case should remain an evaluated intervention rather than a general claim about Mekong agriculture until those details are visible.

◉ Deep dive · Ecological intelligence sits across organism and environment

Priming and timing challenge the habit of locating adaptation inside the organism

The coral and sponge findings are different experiments, but both direct attention to relations. Coral response changes after prior exposure. Sponge metamorphosis changes with an environmental timing cue. Neither mechanism is adequately represented by a static list of traits. The system includes sequence, exposure, signal, and response.

This matters for intervention. Conservation can fail by preserving an organism while degrading the cues and microbial relations through which it acts. It can also fail by turning an interesting laboratory response into a field prescription before duration, cost, and environmental interaction are known. The environment is causal architecture, not backdrop.

Japan's invasive-species amendment adds a governance analogue. Law must detect and act within an ecological interval whose ripeness is set by reproduction and spread, not by the administrative calendar. The Mekong partnership adds an economic analogue: a practice persists only if ecological and household returns remain coupled across seasons.

Design question: Which observations measure the relationship that produces adaptation, rather than only the organism or policy at one point in time?

08 · Liminal Signals

Signals at the Boundary Between Form and System

4 developments
7 Sep 2026 · Greenland and European ArcticSource

One Arctic package makes everyday services strategically legible

The EU–Greenland package announced on 7 September places satellite connectivity, hydropower, critical raw materials, fisheries, housing, tourism, education, and small-business support in one frame. The liminal signal is the disappearance of a clean boundary between strategic infrastructure and ordinary life. A satellite link can be simultaneously a household service, a public-administration tool, and a condition for mineral development.

That Functional Polymorphism changes evaluation. A project cannot be classified by one stated use when the same asset serves several configurations. Benefits and vulnerabilities will be distributed differently across them. Project-level finance, ownership, service obligations, and access terms must therefore be read together. If the portfolio is assessed only through aggregate spending, the component with the easiest metric may stand in for the whole system.

4 Sep 2026 · Marine ecosystemsSource

A nervous-system-free organism turns sunset into developmental timing

The 4 September sponge finding sits at a productive conceptual boundary. Larvae without a nervous system use sunset-associated environmental signals before metamorphosis. The observation resists the assumption that coordinated timing needs a centralized controller.

Distributed control is easy to romanticize. The mechanism may be a narrow photic trigger, and the result depends on controls for wavelength, intensity, temperature, and tides. Still, the case offers a disciplined analogy for institutions and computational systems: coordination can arise from actors responding to a shared environment without one actor representing the whole. Capability Opacity then moves from the center to the coupling. Observers can see the transition while remaining uncertain about which environmental feature carried the decisive information.

8 Sep 2026 · Commercial spaceSource

A billion-dollar close arrives before the reusable vehicle

Stoke's 8 September announcement places a $1 billion initial financing close beside the goal of full launch-vehicle reusability. The funding event is real on its own terms; the technical outcome remains ahead. The liminal signal is the scale at which financial commitment can make an unfinished capability socially present. Hiring, suppliers, facilities, and competitors begin responding before flight evidence settles the engineering claim.

This is not simply narrative replacing reality. Capital changes reality by purchasing tests and time. Yet it also creates a constituency around a particular future. Credential Foreclosure becomes possible if maintaining the valuation story discourages tests that could invalidate the chosen architecture. The clean observable is a transparent milestone sequence in which failure can still redirect design rather than being hidden by aggregate spending.

8 Sep 2026 · Mexico, Paris, and global education systemsSource

On 8 September, UNESCO's literacy observance and its Digital Learning Week placed conventional literacy, media and information literacy, and AI-related knowledge systems on the same public agenda. The boundary matters because a person can read every word in a generated answer and still lack access to provenance, uncertainty, or the means to challenge it. Literacy may therefore acquire relational dimensions: knowing how a claim was produced, which institution warrants it, and when to seek another instrument. If curricula and assessments change, the category is being operationally expanded. If the language stays confined to observances and statements, the old category retains its work while the new risks remain adjacent.

10 · Choice under flux

Wise Action

Actions · vocabulary · research design
Entrepreneurship

Build a milestone ladder before accepting a portfolio claim

For space, climate, education, or infrastructure ventures, separate capital, authority, technical test, customer use, repeat delivery, and system effect. Assign one observable and one owner to each rung. This keeps a large financing event from standing in for engineering, and keeps an official declaration from standing in for implementation.

Markets

Price interfaces as explicit risks

Portfolio announcements often price diversification while hiding dependence among components. Examine whether connectivity depends on one supplier, whether hydropower and extraction share permits, whether AI services depend on one model provider, and whether launch schedules share range or engine bottlenecks. Correlated interfaces can erase the diversification the portfolio appears to provide.

Investment

Ask what the initial close makes irreversible

A large round creates useful runway and path dependence. Identify which facilities, suppliers, hiring commitments, and technical architectures become costly to reverse after capital arrives. Favor programs that preserve learning options and publish evidence capable of invalidating the current design. Optionality is strongest when failure remains informative.

Institutions

Write the common-good claim as a decision right

For education and AI, translate “common good” into procurement transparency, teacher override, student recourse, public data rights, and independent assessment. A principle becomes operational when a named actor can use it to stop or redirect a system. Without that right, the principle remains a rhetorical coordinate.

Research Program Relevance

Connections that alter constructs and observables

5 direct bridges

Coupled action spaces · model the rights inside a portfolio

The EU–Greenland and UNESCO cases sharpen a general design choice: an actor should not choose “the package” as one action when components allocate different rights and consequences. Represent project selection, approval, information access, and veto as separate but coupled moves. The empirical observable is whether a decision in one channel changes feasible moves in another.

Repeated support · distinguish delivery cadence from embedded capacity

China's fourth Nepal shipment changes the operationalization of external support. Count delivery cadence, equipment use, coordination rights, and persistence after emergency demand falls. The mechanism is not aid volume alone; it is whether repetition changes the receiving system's dependencies and available response paths.

Heterogeneous clocks · separate capital, engineering, and institutional time

Isar, Stoke, and the EU budget debate supply three clocks that should not be collapsed. Capital can move immediately, engineering through tests, and institutions through negotiated authority. Track each clock independently and observe when one actor's move changes the ripeness of another's release.

Human–AI ensembles · make verification a complement to autonomy

AI tutors and synthetic knowledge widen the verification burden placed on teachers and learners. A human–AI ensemble should be measured by provenance access, override, error discovery, and recourse, alongside speed or output quality. The boundary condition is whether the human retains usable authority when model capability exceeds local inspection.

Construct validity · preserve the difference between precision and explanation

The supernova catalogue, China PMI decomposition, and Mekong farm claims show why larger or more precise observations do not automatically identify the mechanism. Designs should record alternative interpretations and collect discriminating measurements prospectively. A construct improves when the instrument can rule out a rival, not merely produce a narrower estimate.

Portfolio implications across lenses

1 · A bundle is informative when components can constrain one another

Mechanism: shared authority and measurements turn adjacent programs into a system. Inference limit: common funding can coordinate administration without creating deeper coupling. Discriminating evidence: cross-component vetoes, shared budgets, and decisions changed at an interface.

2 · The observation system can be the limiting infrastructure

Mechanism: catalogues, citizen records, economic subindices, and ecological monitoring determine which differences institutions can act upon. Inference limit: more observations can add noise or selection. Discriminating evidence: replication, correction rights, construct-specific measures, and downstream use.

3 · Timing is part of causation

Mechanism: prior exposure, environmental cues, legal effective dates, financing closes, and meeting windows change what becomes possible next. Inference limit: calendar sequence alone does not identify cause. Discriminating evidence: counterfactual timing, bounded release intervals, and repeated outcomes under altered sequence.

4 · Public purpose needs an actor who can enforce it

Mechanism: common-good, partnership, and comprehensive-policy language acquires substance through rights assigned to named actors. Inference limit: principles can shape behavior before formal enforcement. Discriminating evidence: procurement rules, appeals, approval rights, and resource allocation.
